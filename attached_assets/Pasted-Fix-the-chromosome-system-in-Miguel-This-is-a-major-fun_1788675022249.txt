Each replicated chromosome must have the correct allele labels on BOTH sister chromatids.

* **Yellow chromosome:** `R` and `R` on the corresponding chromosome arms, with the duplicated sister chromatid carrying the same `R` allele.
* **Red chromosome:** `Y` and `Y` on the corresponding chromosome arms, with the duplicated sister chromatid carrying the same `Y` allele.
* The corresponding recessive homologs must carry **`r` and `r`** and **`y` and `y`** respectively.

In other words, do not treat the letters as decorative labels. They must be tied to the underlying identity of the chromosome/chromatid.

* Correctly align the homologous chromosomes before crossing over.
* Perform the crossover between the appropriate non-sister chromatids.
* Reassort the corresponding chromosome segments.
* Update the underlying chromosome/chromatid genetic state.

Do NOT merely animate or swap labels visually. The underlying chromosome data/state must actually change.

**chromosome genetic state → chromatid state → chromosome geometry → allele labels → rendered position**

must remain synchronized.

**Initial chromosomes**
→ **homologous alignment**
→ **crossing-over initiation**
→ **recombinant chromatids**
→ **subsequent stages**
→ **final stage**
